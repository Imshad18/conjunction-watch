2. Publish this run's predictions now

            
Close approaches under 1 km (or with Pc ≥ 1e-5), with CDM files, archived on Zenodo under your name. The DOI timestamps your prediction before the event.

3. Check what happened

            
After each closest approach, newer orbital elements show whether either satellite changed its orbit. An unexplained change right after a predicted
              close approach is evidence of an avoidance manoeuvre, a result independent analysts publish.

            

            

          
4. Publish the outcomes

            
Archives the verification next to your predictions (linked by DOI), building a public track record.
